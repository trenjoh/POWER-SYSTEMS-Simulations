# POWER-SYSTEMS-Simulations

Case Study: Impact of Adding a Shunt Capacitor to Regulate Reactive Power in a Transmission System

This is a simulation based research that focuses on improving the performance of distribution and High Voltage transmission line networks.
The research focuses mainly on adding shunt capacitors parallel to loads with a higher reactive power demand at the buses where they draw the power from.

Find the various findings from my research paper

Few of the things that came out clearly is that by adding this shunt capacitors.
1. line's utility is improved
2. The efficiency of the system improves as well
3. The powerfactor is also improved
4. It improves the line's power carrying capacity.
5.  It also improves the voltage regualtion of the system
